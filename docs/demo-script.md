# AeroGuard AI - Hackathon Demo Script

## Demo Scenario
Aircraft AC-102 Engine Degradation

## Introduction
Aircraft component failures can lead to unexpected maintenance,
operational delays, and increased maintenance costs.

AeroGuard AI is an AI-powered predictive maintenance prototype
that analyzes engine sensor data and estimates Remaining Useful Life.

## Demo Steps

### Step 1: Open Dashboard
Display the aircraft fleet dashboard.

### Step 2: Select Aircraft
Select aircraft AC-102 and its engine ENG-102.

### Step 3: View Sensor Data
Display temperature, vibration, and pressure readings.

### Step 4: Show AI Prediction
Display the predicted Remaining Useful Life (RUL).

### Step 5: Show Risk Alert
Display the predicted risk category and maintenance recommendation.

### Step 6: Simulate Degradation
Use the sensor simulation feature to demonstrate changing
engine health and predicted RUL.

### Step 7: Explain the Benefits
Explain how predictive maintenance can help maintenance
teams prioritize inspections and plan maintenance activities.

## Important Disclaimer
All demonstration aircraft IDs and sensor values are illustrative.
AeroGuard AI is a hackathon prototype and is not certified
for operational aircraft maintenance decisions.
